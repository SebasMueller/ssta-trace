SSTA Workshop 3 · Super-resolution use cases · Metro Manila

Trace the roads you can see

You get two satellite images of residential neighbourhoods in Metro Manila, each 500 m across. Draw a straight line along every road you can make out: press, drag and let go. Then we compare your lines with the real road map.

  1. Image 1: 60 seconds. Draw a line along each road. For a bend, start a new line where the last one ended: it snaps on.
  2. Image 2: another 60 seconds, a different neighbourhood.
  3. Your score: how many of the roads you found on each image. Your lines are added, without your name, to the room's results.
Lines don't have to be perfect. You can stop early with Done.
